## Fact-check report

- **eiffel-location-completion — VERIFIED** (line 3): The official Eiffel Tower site and Wikipedia both state the tower is in Paris, France, and was completed on 31 March 1889.
  - Source: [Eiffel Tower history, architecture, design & construction](https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/the-monument/history)
  - Source: [Eiffel Tower - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eiffel_Tower)
- **water-boiling-point — VERIFIED** (line 5): Britannica and OpenStax both confirm the normal boiling point of water is 100 °C at standard sea-level atmospheric pressure (1 atm).
  - Source: [When does water boil? | Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/question/When-does-water-boil)
  - Source: [1.5 Phase Changes - University Physics Volume 2 | OpenStax](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/1-5-phase-changes)
- **great-wall-visible-space — DISPUTED** (line 7): NASA, Scientific American and Britannica identify this as a myth: the wall cannot be seen from Earth orbit with the naked eye, and astronauts including Yang Liwei reported not seeing it.
  - Source: [Great Wall | NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/great-wall/)
  - Source: [No, You Can't See the Great Wall of China from Space | Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/no-you-cant-see-the-great-wall-of-china-from-space/)
  - Source: [Can you see the Great Wall of China from space? | Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/question/Can-you-see-the-Great-Wall-of-China-from-space)
