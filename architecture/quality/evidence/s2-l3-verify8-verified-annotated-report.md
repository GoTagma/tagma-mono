## Claim details

### C1

- **Draft quote:** "Acme Corporation was founded in 1995 in Zürich, Switzerland."
- **Normalized claim:** Acme Corporation was founded in 1995.
- **Verdict:** unknown
- **Reason:** "Acme Corporation" is a generic/fictional placeholder name (Wikipedia describes it as a fictional corporation used as a running gag and as a generic business title). Searches surfaced several unrelated real entities named Acme (ACME CORP. AG in Switzerland, incorporated 26 Aug 1994; Acme United Corporation, roots to 1867; Acme World Sports, created 1995 in the US). No authoritative source substantiates a generic "Acme Corporation" founded in 1995. No adequate evidence found; not refuted.

- **Normalized claim:** Acme Corporation was founded in Zürich, Switzerland.
- **Verdict:** unknown

### C3

- **Draft quote:** "The Eiffel Tower is 330 metres tall and was completed in 1889."
- **Normalized claim:** The Eiffel Tower is 330 metres tall.
- **Verdict:** supported
- **Evidence:**
  - "Tip | 330 m (1,083 ft)" — Wikipedia - Eiffel Tower (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eiffel_Tower)
  - "Height: 330 meters (1,083 feet)" — Encyclopaedia Britannica - Eiffel Tower (https://www.britannica.com/topic/Eiffel-Tower-Paris-France)
- **Notes:** Multiple independent authoritative sources give the Eiffel Tower's current tip height as 330 metres, matching the claim's metric, entity and unit.

### C4

- **Verdict:** supported
